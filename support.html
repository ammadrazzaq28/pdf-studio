Frequently asked questions

Is my data stored on your servers?

No. All document processing — scanning, conversion, OCR, merge, split, and compression — runs entirely on your iPhone. We do not upload, store, or access your files on any server.

How do I convert a Word file to PDF?

Open File Converter, go to Convert, select Word to PDF, pick your file from Files or iCloud, and tap Convert. The output is saved on your device.

Why can’t the app access my camera or photos?

Go to Settings → File Converter and enable Camera and Photos. Then return to the app and try again.

How do I scan a document?

Tap Scan on the home screen, point your camera at the document, and capture. The app auto-detects edges and lets you export as PDF.

How do I merge or split PDFs?

Go to Tools, select Merge or Split, choose your PDF files, and follow the on-screen steps. All processing happens on your device.

How do I cancel File Converter Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → File Converter → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the subscription screen in the App or in Settings, tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

What does the 3-day free trial include?

Eligible new subscribers receive File Converter Pro access for three days. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Apple automatically bills the weekly subscription shown on the paywall.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

How do I clear the app cache?

Go to Settings in the app, scroll to Storage, and tap Cleanup Cache. This removes temporary files but does not delete your saved documents.

Still need help?

Include your iOS version, App version, and a short description of the issue when you email us.
