1. Introduction

PDF Studio (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Important: All document processing — including scanning, conversion, OCR, merge, split, and compression — runs entirely on your iPhone. We do not upload, store, or access your documents, files, or their contents on our servers or any cloud service operated by us.

By downloading or using PDF Studio, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. On-device processing — we do not store your data

PDF Studio is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your documents and files are processed locally on your device using on-device technology. This means:

  • We do not store your documents. Your PDFs, Word files, images, scans, and converted outputs are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not have access to your file contents. We cannot read, view, or retrieve the documents you process in the App.
  • No account is required. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use core App features.
  • No content logging. We do not log the contents of your documents or conversion history on any server.
  • No AI training. Your files are never sent to an AI model for training or analysis.

All files you create or import remain on your iPhone until you delete them or uninstall the App.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information stored on your device only

When you use PDF Studio, the App may store locally on your iPhone:

  • Documents, scans, and converted files you create or import
  • Conversion history and recent activity
  • App preferences — appearance, language, default quality, and compression settings
  • Subscription status needed to unlock premium features
  • Temporary cache files used during conversion (you can clear these in Settings)

None of this data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

4.2 Device permissions

PDF Studio may request access to:

  • Camera — to scan documents and capture images for PDF conversion
  • Photo library — to import images and save exported documents

You can revoke permissions at any time in iPhone Settings → PDF Studio.

4.3 Purchases & subscriptions

If you subscribe to PDF Studio Pro, payment is processed by Apple. We receive subscription status and related receipt information needed to unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

4.4 Analytics & advertising

We may use third-party services that collect limited technical or usage information, such as:

  • Google AdMob — if you are not subscribed, the App may show ads; AdMob may collect device and ad interaction data according to Google’s policies
  • Device type, operating system version, and App version
  • Crash or performance data if you opt in to sharing diagnostics with developers via Apple

These services do not receive your document contents or file data.

4.5 App Tracking Transparency

On supported iOS versions, we may ask for permission to track your device across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising measurement. If you deny permission, we respect your choice and do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) for that purpose.

5. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide scanning, conversion, OCR, merge, split, compression, and related features on your device
  • Validate and manage PDF Studio Pro subscriptions
  • Display ads to non-subscribers and measure ad performance when permitted
  • Improve reliability, security, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not store your documents or file contents.

6. Storage & retention

All documents, scans, preferences, and conversion history remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We do not retain copies on our servers because we never receive them.

Subscription records are retained as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting requirements.

If you contact support, we retain correspondence for a reasonable period to resolve your request.

7. Sharing & third parties

We may share limited information with:

  • Apple Inc. — App Store purchases and system services
  • Google (AdMob) — advertising as described above (no document content is shared)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not share your documents, file contents, or conversion data with any third party.

8. App tracking & ads

You can control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. Subscribers may see fewer or no ads depending on App configuration. Denying tracking does not block free features that do not require a subscription, subject to feature limits shown in the App.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent (for example, camera or tracking permissions) at any time via device settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EEA/UK users)

Because all App data and documents are stored on your device, you can remove them by deleting the App or clearing cache in Settings. For subscription management, use Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell personal information as defined by California law.

10. Children

PDF Studio is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. International users

If you use the App outside your home country, your documents remain on your device. Limited technical data may be processed by Apple or advertising providers in accordance with their terms. By using the App, you understand that those providers may process data in countries where they operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the App where appropriate.
